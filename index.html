About

Where rigor meets vision.

I am a statistician turned data scientist and a founder. I hold a B.Sc. (Honors) in Statistics from the University of Zimbabwe and am completing an M.S. in Data Science at the University of Colorado Boulder (December 2026).

My work spans applied machine learning, causal inference, and time-series forecasting, from public-health survival analysis to revenue analytics. Beyond the lab, I built and led The Dew Revolution, taking a bottled drink from idea to market.

Next, I am pursuing a PhD in Biostatistics, focused on infectious-disease-associated cancers and the statistics that can improve patient outcomes.
